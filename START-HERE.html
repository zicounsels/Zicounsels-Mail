Your email workspace starts here.

Compass Mail brings your audience, branded emails, scheduling and follow-up journeys into one staff workspace.

This is a deployable first release. Your accounts must be connected and tested before it can send mail. It includes 36 templates and eight journey recipes; it does not reproduce every Mailchimp feature.
  1. Upload the source to GitHub. Put the contents of this folder at the repository root.
  2. Connect Vercel. Choose Other as the framework and public as the output directory.
  3. Follow the full deployment guide. It walks through Google Cloud, the contact Sheet, staff sign-in, sending-domain verification and scheduled delivery.
  4. Test with yourself first. Complete the acceptance checklist before using your customer list.

Files to read

  • Deployment guide — step-by-step commands and settings
  • Staff guide — how your team runs campaigns
  • Feature coverage — what works and what comes next
  • Verification checklist — local tests and first-recipient acceptance

The Markdown guides can be read directly on GitHub or in any text editor. JavaScript dependencies are recorded in package.json and package-lock.json; npm ci installs them automatically.

To preview the staff interface locally, run python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory public in this folder and open http://localhost:8080. Do not double-click the app HTML: browser security may block its template loading.
